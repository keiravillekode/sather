# Design

## Goal

Introduce `INT`, routine arguments, and the five arithmetic operators, with
particular attention to `/` and `%` as a pair.

## Learning objectives

- Declare and use routine arguments.
- Use `+`, `-`, `*`, `/` and `%` on `INT`.
- Predict what integer division does with a remainder.

## Out of scope

- `FLTD`. Tasks 3 and 4 are chosen so that integer division is the right
  answer rather than a limitation to apologise for.
- `.str`. Nothing here builds a sentence, so numbers never meet strings.
- Negative-number edge cases in `%`, which are not needed for the story.

- `numbers`
